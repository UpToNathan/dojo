Authored UI and syntax mappings in src/theme/authored-presets.txt additionally use:

- omacom/omarchy, `themes/{osaka-jade,miasma,ristretto,flexoki-light,matte-black}/colors.toml`,
  revision `4c1b4cb3a94447aff39af98e2fcc0490a9fb32e7` (quattro). Semantic source
  colors are retained as preset inputs; Dojo's ANSI slots are adapted from the
  named normal/bright colors. Omarchy wallpaper assets are not included.

- folke/tokyonight.nvim, cdc07ac78467a233fd62c493de29a17e0cf2b2b6.
  Palette values generated with colors.setup({style=..., light_style="night"}).
  The source palette values are retained; Dojo assigns them to shared app roles.
  Licensed under Apache-2.0; see tokyonight-Apache-2.0.txt.
- catppuccin/palette, 07d02aa110ef9eb7e7427afca5c73ba9cf7f8ebd, palette.json.
  Dojo assigns named colors to UI/syntax roles and blends diff backgrounds.
