Hokum for Wayland compositors with `zwlr_layer_shell_v1` (niri, sway,
Hyprland), for Linux x86_64 and aarch64.

- A new settings window: a sidebar with Appearance, Behaviour and
  Applications, a page per app (mute, how long its cards stay, breaking
  through Do not disturb), and screens that switch instantly. Appearance picks
  one of the built-in themes, or your own from `~/.config/hokum/themes/`.
- Custom themes are written by your coding agent: **Make your own theme**
  under Appearance gives you a prompt to copy that points it at
  https://hokum.app, the complete guide to the theme format. The look
  controls, per-app colour and layout, and **Clone to edit** are gone from the
  window; `tokens`, `template` and rules in `config.kdl` still work.
- Breaking change for custom themes: `behaviour` (position, how long cards
  stay, how many show, pause on hover, Do not disturb, appearance) is set only
  in `config.kdl`. A theme with a `behaviour` block is reported as a problem;
  move that block into your `config.kdl`.
- `hokum check [path]` reports problems in `config.kdl`, or in a theme or
  config file, and exits non-zero if there are any.
- A rule's `template "…"` can name a template from the theme, any theme it
  starts from, or the config itself.
- Clicking a notification now works on the first click, and the app can raise
  its window: Hokum sends an XDG activation token (`ActivationToken`) before
  `ActionInvoked`, which Slack and other libnotify/Electron apps use on
  Wayland.
- The first time Hokum runs, it shows one welcome card with an
  **Open Settings** button.
