# dojo-terminal on Rune

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 01

A Rune app that draws a `dojo-rex` terminal as a cell grid with a cursor, sends keys through the key encoder, follows resizes, and exits on `child_exited`. `--panel` opens a layer-shell overlay at an output, margins and size; the default opens a normal window. `--session LABEL` attaches or creates.
